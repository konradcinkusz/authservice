# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a straightforwardly positive experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Things that make this project work:

- Being kind to other people, and assuming they are acting in good faith.
- Giving and accepting review feedback about the code rather than the person.
- Saying "I was wrong" when you were, and moving on.
- Being patient with people who are new to the codebase, to .NET, or to open source.

Things that are not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or persistently re-litigating settled decisions.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages —
and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers by opening an issue if it can be discussed
publicly, or by contacting a maintainer directly if it cannot.

Maintainers will review every report, keep the reporter's identity confidential, and respond
in a way proportionate to what happened — which may mean a correction, a warning, a temporary
block, or a permanent ban. Maintainers who do not act in good faith may face consequences
determined by other maintainers.
